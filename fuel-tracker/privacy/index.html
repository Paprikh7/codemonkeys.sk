Privacy policy

Last change: 25 September 2026

Code Monkeys s. r. o. makes the iPhone app Fuel Tracker. This policy tells you which data the app uses, where the data stays, and which rights you have.

  • Fuel Tracker does not collect personal data.
  • Your receipts and your records stay on your iPhone.
  • The app has no user account, no advertising, and no tracking.
  • We get data from you only when you send it to us.

Data on your iPhone

The app keeps this data in its own storage on your iPhone:

  • the photos of your receipts
  • the text that the app reads from the photos
  • your fill-up records: the date, the station, the liters, the price per liter, the total, and the odometer value if you enter it

The app reads the receipts on your iPhone. It uses the text recognition of Apple (the Vision framework). On iPhones that support it, the app also uses the on-device language model of Apple. The photos and the text do not leave your iPhone.

When you delete the app, iOS deletes all of its data.

Camera and photos

The app asks for access to the camera, so that you can take a photo of a receipt. You can turn this access off at any time in the Settings app of iOS.

To use a photo that you already have, you select it in the photo picker of iOS. The app then gets only the photos that you select.

iCloud Backup

If iCloud Backup is on, iOS adds the data of the app to your backup. Apple keeps the backup under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot see your backup.

Data that you send us

You decide when data leaves the app. For example, you can export your records, or you can send us a receipt that the app did not read correctly. In both cases, iOS opens the share sheet, and you choose where the data goes.

If you send us an email or a receipt, we use it only to answer you and to improve how the app reads receipts. We delete it when we no longer need it for that purpose, and at the latest after 12 months. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering you and in improving the app (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Our email service is iCloud Mail by Apple. We do not sell data, and we do not give it to other companies.

Crash reports and statistics from Apple

If you agree to share analytics with app developers, Apple sends us crash reports and usage statistics that do not show your name. You control this in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements.

If you test a beta version with TestFlight, Apple shares your TestFlight data with us. That data includes your email address, the number of sessions, crash reports, and the feedback that you send.

No tracking and no advertising

The app does not track you across apps or websites. It contains no advertising and no analytics code from other companies.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have these rights for your personal data:

  • to get a copy of it
  • to correct it
  • to delete it
  • to limit its use, or to object to its use
  • to get it in a format that a machine can read

Most of your data never reaches us, so you control it in the app. For data that you sent us, write to info@codemonkeys.sk.

You can also complain to the Slovak supervisory authority: Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky, Galvaniho 7/B, 821 04 Bratislava, dataprotection.gov.sk.

Changes to this policy

When the app starts to use data in a new way, we update this page and the date at the top.
